    How to count by voice

    Use short, natural phrases. A brief pause separates items automatically, or say next, bump, or then while counting continuously.

    1
    Choose where you are counting

    Select the starting location, put in your headset, and tap Start counting.

    2
    Say item + quantity + unit

    Use the item names and units configured in inventory.

    “Chicken breast, two cases.”
    3
    Separate each item

    Pause briefly, or use a transition word when you do not want to stop talking.

    “Chicken breast two cases, next tomatoes five pounds, bump onions three pounds.”
    4
    Listen for confirmation

    The app plays one confirmation tone per saved item. A spoken question or warning means an entry needs clarification or review.

    Count“Tomatoes five pounds.”
    Several“Tomatoes five pounds, next onions three pounds, bump carrots two bags.”
    Transitions“Next,” “next item,” “bump,” or “then.”
    Add“Add chicken breast one case.”
    Correct“Change chicken breast three cases.”
    Remove“Remove mozzarella sticks.”
    Location“Switch to freezer.”
    Pause“Pause inventory.”
    Finish“Finish inventory.”
    Important: If you count the same item again, say add or change. Otherwise, the app will ask which action you intended. After a voice pause, tap Resume when you are ready.
